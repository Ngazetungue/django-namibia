About Django Namibia

The official community for Django developers in Namibia, fostering collaboration, mentorship, and knowledge sharing.

Our Story

How It All Began

Django Namibia was founded in 2016 by a group of passionate developers under the umbrella of the Python Software Association of Namibia (PyNAM). We recognized the need for a dedicated Django community in Namibia.

What started as informal meetups in Windhoek has grown into a national community with members across the country. Our first official event brought together 30 developers, and since then we've been organizing workshops, hackathons, and networking events to grow the Django ecosystem in Namibia.

Our PyNAM Affiliation

Django Namibia is an initiative of the Python Software Association of Namibia (PyNAM), serving as the country's official Django community. PyNAM connects us with the broader Python community while letting us focus on Django development.

Learn more about PyNAM →

Our Growth

With PyNAM support, we've grown to 200+ members across Namibia, including beginners, experienced Django developers, and companies building products with Django.

We've partnered with local tech companies, educational institutions, and the global Django community to bring world-class resources and opportunities to Namibian developers.
