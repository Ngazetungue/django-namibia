Community Code of Conduct

Our shared commitment to creating a welcoming, respectful environment for all Django enthusiasts in Namibia

By participating in Django Namibia, you're helping build a space where everyone can thrive, learn, and contribute without fear of harassment or discrimination.

Our Community Promise

Guided by the principles of Ubuntu and Namibia's rich diversity, we are committed to creating an inclusive space where every participant, no matter their background or skill level, feels respected and empowered. Whether you're a student in Windhoek, a professional in Swakopmund, or connecting with us virtually, you have a home here.

We Embrace

  • Kindness and constructive feedback
  • Diverse perspectives and experiences
  • Collaboration over competition

We Reject

  • Harassment in any form
  • Discriminatory language or behavior
  • Exclusionary practices

Your Safety Matters

If you experience or witness misconduct: You're not alone. Our response team is here to help discreetly. Reports can be made anonymously if preferred.

Namibian Context

We recognize Namibia's unique cultural diversity. Our conduct standards specifically address:

  • Respect for all ethnic groups and languages
  • Sensitivity to historical inequalities
  • Encouraging participation from underrepresented groups
